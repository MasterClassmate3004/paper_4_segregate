WHAT THE SPLITTER DOES
  It cuts the MS by its printed keys ("1(c)(ii)", "(b)(i)", "2 (a)") into one crop per question-paper LEAF (the QP labels come
  from the approved QP split). Mapping per QP leaf: exact key; or the nearest MS ancestor key covers the leaf
  (parent_ms_covers_child_qp); or MS keys below the leaf stacked (children_ms_merged_for_parent_qp); else UNRESOLVED.
  An MS key no leaf uses is an ORPHAN. The paper passes only with no unresolved, no orphans, no empty leaves, and a clean gate:
  ms_gate.log "missing=0 duplicated=0 sliced=0 overlaps=0 furniture=0".

OVERRIDE FORMAT (JSON; every override needs a "reason" saying what is printed and why)
  "add_keys":   {"Q1.i": [page, y_top_of_printed_line, x_right_edge_of_printed_label]}   a key the text layer lost or misprinted
  "drop_keys":  ["Q5.c"]                                   discard automatic keys (and their descendants)
  "segments":   {"Q5.c.i": [[page, y0, y1], ...]}          cut a key's rows by hand (y in points, on blank rows)
  "masks":      [[page, x0, y0, x1, y1]]                   white out a printed label inside a hand segment
  "absent":     {"Q1.a": "reason"}                         a QP leaf the published MS genuinely does not mark
  "page_bottom": {"7": 798}                                content printed in a scan's bottom 70 pt (otherwise taken as footer)
  "full_width_pages": [3]                                  an answer table spanning the Question column: crop from its left rule
  "keep_words": [[page, x0, y0]]                           a word in the label column that is marking text, not a repeated key
  "skip_pages": [1]                                        pages that are not marking content (e.g. a scanned cover)
  Pages are 1-based. Look at existing examples in the same folder (AL-PHY-*-MS.json, AL-CHE-25s-52-MS.json).

RULES
  - Never invent marking content. A misprinted key (e.g. "1(h)(i)" printed where 1(i) belongs, "4(c)(iv)" for the QP's
    (c)(iii)) is fixed with add_keys at the printed line, and the reason names the misprint.
  - Never assign unlabelled or numbered marking points to QP sub-parts by your own reading: when the MS does not print
    the sub-part labels, keep the parent whole (its segment covers all the points) so every sub-part sees the whole scheme.
  - Use "absent" only when the MS truly has no marking point for that leaf (say so in the reason).
  - When the MS is organised differently from the QP (e.g. old Paper 5 by skill), keep the question whole with drop_keys +
    segments on the question number so every leaf gets the whole question's scheme (see AL-PHY-02s-51-MS.json).
  - Garbled text layers (duplicated glyphs like "(i ii)") or scans: place keys by hand from the picture.
  - Each crop must show only its own marking points, no key labels, no page header/footer, nothing sliced.
